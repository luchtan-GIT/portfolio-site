## About

*Public Instruments* is a long-form research project documenting musical instruments installed in public spaces globally. The project explores who builds them, who plays them, how communities form around them, and what it means to make music in the open.

The work draws on fieldwork, archival research, and interviews, and is intended as a reference work and cultural study of a largely undocumented phenomenon.

## Scope

The book aims to catalogue between 50 and 100 instruments across diverse geographic, cultural, and institutional contexts — from urban sound installations to rural community instruments.
