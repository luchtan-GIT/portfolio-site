## About

*Materials Moderns, Textures Antigues* (Modern Materials, Ancient Textures) is a work for acoustic instruments and electronics, composed for and premiered at the Campanar de Santa Maria del Pi in Barcelona.

The piece explores the dialogue between contemporary sound-making and the acoustic character of a medieval stone space — the long reverb, the irregular surfaces, the weight of the architecture.

## Performance

Premiered at Santa Maria del Pi, Barcelona. Program notes and libretto available on request.
