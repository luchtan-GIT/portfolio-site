## About

Lonesome Daze is an original music project rooted in bluegrass and American folk traditions, with a contemporary songwriter's ear. Songs are written and performed primarily on fiddle and guitar.

Based in Barcelona, the project draws on both the deep tradition of old-time and bluegrass music and the experience of living and making music in Europe.
