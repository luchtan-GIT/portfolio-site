## Overview

*Peripheral Oscillator Topology* is an ongoing research paper at the intersection of complexity science and ethnomusicology. The work uses qualitative methods — loosely grounded in grounded theory — to examine how oscillator networks at the periphery of musical systems behave and interact.

The paper situates complexity thinking within an ethnographic framework, exploring how these theoretical models map onto real musical practices and communities.
